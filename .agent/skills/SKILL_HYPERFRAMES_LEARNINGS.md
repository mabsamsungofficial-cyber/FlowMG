# Skill: HyperFrames & Reverse-Engineered Video Motion Architecture

## Overview
Synthesized from HeyGen's `hyperframes` open-source framework and reverse-engineered from high-retention vertical explainers (HisaabKitaab101 & MrBeast Breakdown). This skill defines how to translate reference video aesthetics and narrative scripts into programmatic 2D/3D animated motion graphics.

---

## 1. Core Principles from HeyGen HyperFrames

### A. The Motion Doctrine & The Vector Law
- **No Idle Wobble:** Elements never loop arbitrary sine-wave breathing or idle floats when a scene is running. Every frame must **perform** narrative work (a staged reveal, a line drawing, a metric counting up, or an element moving to dock).
- **The Vector Law across Seams:** The direction an element or scene exits must match the incoming element's vector (same axis, same directional sign).
- **Causal Motion:** Every motion is launched by a visible cause (Audio word beat $\to$ scale pop $\to$ connecting dashed line extends $\to$ next card triggers).
- **Staged Reveals:** Information enters sequentially across spoken beats rather than popping in all at once.

### B. Captions Overlay Doctrine (Rail vs. Embed)
- **The Rail (Verbatim Lower-Third Subtitle):** Clean dark rounded pill at bottom center (`#0F172A` with 80% opacity), holding 3–6 spoken words at a time with active words illuminated in gold/yellow/green.
- **The Kinetic Punch (Embed / Apex Beat):** High-impact words (`मस्त PROMOTION`, `VIDEO चली = VIEWS`, `5 STEP PLAN`) promoted to large, bold center headlines with vibrant red underlines or cursive italic highlights.

## 2. Reverse-Engineered Visual Blueprints

### Style 1: HisaabKitaab Storytelling Explainer (Reference Video 1)
- **Palette:** Warm cream paper canvas (`#FAF7F0` / `#F5F1E8`), soft charcoal text (`#1E293B`), warm gold/amber accents (`#D97706`).
- **Top Financial Category Navigation Bar:** Rounded pill tabs across the top:
  `[Assets]` `[Liabilities]` `[Equity]` `[Income]` `[Expenses]` with the current topic highlighted in solid gold/amber.
- **Center Scene Composition:** Illustrated 2D stall / shop environment with floating transaction receipts and unit cost breakdown cards (`Thele ke liye chahiye Rs 3,000`, `Zaid ke paas Rs 2,000`).
- **Captions:** Bottom-centered rounded dark badge with active word colored in gold.

### Style 2: 5-Step Plan & Audience Matrix Explainer (Reference Video 2)
- **Palette:** Off-white grid paper (`#F8F9FA` with 30px technical grid), high-contrast Black headlines (`#0F172A`), vibrant Crimson Red (`#E11D48` / `#FF2A3A`).
- **Top Metadata Tag:** Monospace chapter index at top-left: `// 01 · MrBeast`, `// 02 · brand`, `// 04 · asli khel`.
- **Center Visual Cards:**
  1. **5-Step Numbered Flowchart:** Red & dark number badges (`[ 1 ] ₹500 मदद`, `[ 2 ] पूरी VIDEO`, `[ 3 ] BRAND`, `[ 4 ] SPONSOR`, `[ 5 ] VIEWS`) connected by animated dashed pink/red paths.
  2. **Tilted 3D Smartphone Mockup:** Isometric black smartphone with screen recording UI (`● REC 00:00`, floating currency note).
  3. **Audience Conversion Matrix:** 10x8 grid of person icons where target viewer percentages ignite in crimson red (`VIDEO चली = VIEWS`).
  4. **Kinetic Typography:** Ultra-bold uppercase sans-serif with red underline rules and cursive italic counterpoint words.
- **Narrator Avatar:** Casual creator with curly dark hair, black beard, stylish glasses, dark blue t-shirt, gesturing upwards and talking in real-time lip-sync.
